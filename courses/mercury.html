Who is Mercury for?

Mercury is a Cambridge Math class taught entirely in English. Children learn mathematical thinking — bar models, ratios, percent, multi-step problems — and the language of mathematics (estimate, quotient, compare) so they can explain their thinking clearly.

  • Students who do well in math at school and want to learn the vocabulary and the way to present solutions in English.
  • Students who enjoy challenging problems that don't follow a ready-made pattern.
  • Families who want their child to learn English through content, not just word lists.
  • Students who want to get to know the Cambridge Math curriculum.
Not sure which level fits?

The placement test is a set of problems that identifies your child's gaps and strengths by topic; the results decide the right group and level of support.

Why learn math in English?

Mercury applies the principle of learning language through content: children use English to do real work — solve problems, explain and defend their solutions — so vocabulary and sentence structures attach to meaning instead of being memorized. Every lesson has diagrams, concrete objects and sentence frames to support the language; at the end of a unit, children give a 60–90 second oral solution with steps and checks.   • ≥75% on end‑term problem set

Educational journey
  1. Entry: placement set identifies topic gaps and strengths.
  2. Unit A: number sense + bar models; daily reasoning prompts; micro‑presentations.
  3. Unit B: ratios/percent; multi‑step problems; reflection logs.
  4. Checkpoint: cumulative review; error‑pattern reteach; second presentation.
  5. Next: Cambridge checkpoint prep or transfer to science projects.

Who teaches your child?

The program is led by a teacher with a New Jersey (USA) teaching certificate, who teaches to the US InTASC professional standards for teachers — the specific criteria are listed in each section of this page.

HEC has trained foreign and Vietnamese teaching assistants with degrees in English or education, who support small-group discussion, marking and demanding tasks. Research from the Education Endowment Foundation (UK) found that oral language work led by trained teaching assistants is about as effective as teacher-led work.

FAQ

Who is the Mercury class for?

Students who want to learn Cambridge Math in English: getting to know math vocabulary, solving non-routine word problems and explaining their thinking. HEC groups students after a free entry test.

Will learning math in English be too hard to follow?

The class uses diagrams, concrete objects and sentence frames to support the language, and moves from conceptual models to procedures. The entry test identifies gaps and strengths so your child is placed at a suitable level.

What will my child achieve after Mercury?

At least 75% on the end-of-term problem set; precise use of math vocabulary (quotient, estimate, compare); a 60–90 second oral solution with steps and checks.

What comes after Mercury?

Cambridge checkpoint preparation, or a move to science projects.
