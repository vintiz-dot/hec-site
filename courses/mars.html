Who is Mars for?

Mars is the first level of HEC's ELA pathway, for kindergarten children from age 4. Children learn the foundations of reading and writing: recognizing letters and sounds, blending sounds to read three-letter words, listening to and retelling stories, then saying 6–8 sentences about themselves and their family.

  • Children from age 4 who don't know the English letters yet, or are just getting to know them.
  • Children who love stories, songs and play but can't read words by themselves yet.
  • Families who want their child to love English first, and learn grammar later.
  • Parents who want to track progress against specific goals: decoding three-letter words with 90%+ accuracy, reading aloud 30–40 words per minute, speaking 6–8 sentences.
Not sure which class fits?

The 10-minute placement check (letter names, sounds, print concepts) is free. HEC places children by level, not just by age, and will say so plainly if your child should start elsewhere.

How is an ELA class for kindergarten different from a regular English class?

Many kindergarten classes only sing, play and repeat single words. In Mars, games and songs are still the way in, but the goal is real reading and writing foundations: explicit phonics with cumulative review, choral and echo reading for fluency, say–tap–write routines and first decodable readers. At the end, each child has a recorded reading sample and a progress folder shared with the family.

Essential questions
  • How do pictures and sounds help me read?

Intended outcomes
  • Alphabet + phonemic awareness
  • ~200 high‑frequency words
  • Simple “I am / I have / This is …” sentences

Success criteria
  • Decode CVC ≥90% accuracy
  • Read aloud 30–40 wpm with phrasing
  • Speak 6–8 sentences about self/family
  • Write 4 correct sentences with capitals/spacing
Educational journey
  1. Entry: 10‑minute screen checks letter names, sounds and print concepts.
  2. Weeks 1–2: routines, letter formation, oral blending; first decodables.
  3. Weeks 3–4: CVC mastery; picture word banks; first recorded reading sample.
  4. Weeks 5–6: sentence frames for show‑and‑tell; home reading logs begin.
  5. Weeks 7–8: cumulative review; small‑group acceleration for advanced readers.
  6. End: fluency + phonics inventory; share progress folder with parents.
  7. Next: move to Look 1 (Venus) with individual sight‑word targets.

Who teaches your child?

The program is led by a teacher with a New Jersey (USA) teaching certificate, who teaches to the US InTASC professional standards for teachers — the specific criteria are listed in each section of this page.

HEC has trained foreign and Vietnamese teaching assistants with degrees in English or education, who support small-group discussion, marking and demanding tasks. Research from the Education Endowment Foundation (UK) found that oral language work led by trained teaching assistants is about as effective as teacher-led work.

FAQ

What age can my child join the Mars class?

Mars is for kindergarten children from age 4. HEC places children by level with a free placement check of about 10 minutes (letter names, sounds and print concepts), not by age alone.

My child doesn't know any letters yet. Can they still join?

Yes. Mars starts with classroom routines, letter formation and oral blending, using real photos and first decodable readers; children don't need to read beforehand.

What will my child achieve after Mars?

Decode three-letter (CVC) words with at least 90% accuracy; read aloud 30–40 words per minute; speak 6–8 sentences about themselves and their family; write 4 correct sentences with capitals and spacing. These are learning goals checked throughout the course.

Wednesdays and Fridays, 19:30–21:00. The fee is 210,000 VND per 90-minute session, about 1,680,000–1,890,000 VND a month, billed only for sessions held, with a 5% sibling discount and currently no book fee.

Which class comes after Mars?

Venus (National Geographic Look 1), with individual sight-word targets for each child; fast readers can be accelerated in small groups.
