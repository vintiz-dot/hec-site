Who is the Sun IELTS class for?

Sun is HEC's IELTS class: 12-week cycles, once a week, 2 hours a session. The class is built on ELA — reading academic texts, writing arguments with evidence, presenting and discussing an issue — so students prepare for IELTS with real ability, not just test tricks.

  • High-school students (Grades 10–12) who need structured IELTS preparation on a manageable schedule.
  • Students who need Writing and Speaking marked and corrected closely against the band descriptors in a small class.
  • A target of band 5.5–7.0 and a 0.5-band gain in 12–16 weeks for students who follow the full program (a learning target, not a score guarantee).
  • Families who want transparent per-session fees with nothing paid for the whole course upfront.
Is your child under 16?

IELTS is not recommended for candidates under 16. If your child is younger, build the ELA foundation first with SKY 1 or Sanrio Grade 6 — see IELTS classes in Đống Đa.

Why is the IELTS class built on ELA?

Writing Task 2 is an argument essay, Reading is long academic text, Speaking Part 3 is reasoned discussion — all of them are ELA skills at a higher level. So the Sun class opens with a four-skill diagnostic, drills each sub-skill under time limits, then integrates: two section mocks and two full mocks in every 12-week cycle, with spoken and written feedback.

Who teaches your child?

The program is led by a teacher with a New Jersey (USA) teaching certificate, who teaches to the US InTASC professional standards for teachers — the specific criteria are listed in each section of this page.

HEC has trained foreign and Vietnamese teaching assistants with degrees in English or education, who support small-group discussion, marking and demanding tasks. Research from the Education Endowment Foundation (UK) found that oral language work led by trained teaching assistants is about as effective as teacher-led work.

FAQ

Who is the Sun IELTS class for?

High-school students who need structured IELTS preparation on a manageable schedule and want Writing and Speaking marked closely against the band descriptors in a class of up to 12. Target band 5.5–7.0.

Is one session a week enough?

Once a week for 2 hours is designed to fit around high school; every session has timed tasks and students get a self-study plan from the opening diagnostic. A 0.5-band gain in 12–16 weeks is a learning target, not a score guarantee.

Who marks Writing and Speaking in the Sun class?

A teacher with a New Jersey (USA) teaching certificate marks against the published band descriptors with spoken and written feedback; the class also has its own Speaking mock-test tool.

How do the 2026–2027 IELTS rules affect us?

In the 2026 graduation exam IELTS 4.0+ exempted students from the foreign-language paper (not counted as a 10); 2026 university admission caps bonus points for language certificates at 1.5, and a 2027 draft proposes removing them. Details and sources are in our guide to IELTS classes in Đống Đa.
