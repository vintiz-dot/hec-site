An ELA school, not another ESL class

HEC – Happy English Club is an English Language Arts (ELA) center in Đống Đa, Hanoi. Most English classes teach children about English: word lists, grammar rules, set phrases. We teach children to use English — to read real books, write with reasons, discuss, debate and present, the way students in American schools do.

Our curriculum follows the US Common Core ELA standards and Cambridge English levels, built on National Geographic Look and Reach Higher, Oxford Discover and Cambridge Math, with levels that match the CEFR — from kindergarten (age 4) to Grade 12, with IELTS for older students. Classes are led by a US-licensed teacher with trained foreign and Vietnamese teaching assistants, and capped at 12 students, so every child is heard every lesson.

Beyond the classroom, our free Saturday Reading Club is open to all ages, and our Summer Camp offers full-English immersion. Families follow along through our own student portal — homework, attendance, points, and progress, all in one place.

Fees are transparent and per-session: 210,000₫ for a 90-minute class, 260,000₫ for 120-minute IELTS. You're billed only for sessions actually held, with a 5% sibling discount and no hidden fees.

ELA vs ESL: Why HEC Teaches English Differently

Most English classes teach a list of words and rules (ESL). HEC teaches English Language Arts: English as a language to read, think and speak in, aligned to the US Common Core.

A typical ESL class

  • Memorize word lists by topic
  • Grammar rules and gap-fill exercises
  • Repeat model dialogues
  • Tests of vocabulary and grammar

An HEC ELA class

  • Read real stories and science texts
  • Write paragraphs and essays in every genre
  • Discuss, debate and present with evidence
  • Rubrics for writing and speaking, measurable goals
