Where every child finds their voice

HEC – Happy English Club is a small-class English center in Đống Đa District, Hanoi, built around one belief: children learn to speak English when they're confident enough to try.

Every class starts with a Big Question. Our Grade 2–6 students learn through project-based, inquiry-driven lessons built on Oxford Discover, National Geographic Look, and Cambridge Math — taught by a US-licensed teacher certified by Cambridge and Oxford. With a maximum of 12 students per class, every child gets heard, every lesson.

Beyond the classroom, our free Saturday Reading Club is open to all ages, and our Summer Camp offers full-English immersion. Families follow along through our own student portal — homework, attendance, points, and progress, all in one place.

Fees are transparent and per-session: 210,000₫ for a 90-minute class, 260,000₫ for 120-minute IELTS. You're billed only for sessions actually held, with a 5% sibling discount and no hidden fees.
