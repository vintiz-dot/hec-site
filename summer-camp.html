A Day at HEC Camp

The same rhythm that produced last summer's results — full-day, full-English, and built around how children actually learn.

08:00 — 08:15
Mindfulness & Check-in

A calm brain learns faster. Daily emotional check-ins build focus before the first lesson begins.

08:15 — 10:00
Active English (PBL)

Project-based English: asking big questions, debating, reading and presenting — not memorising.

10:10 — 11:00
Engineering & Robotics

Blueprinting, building and stress-testing real prototypes — knowledge that passes through the hands stays in the mind.

11:00 — 14:00
Lunch, Rest & Recharge

Nutritious lunch, a proper nap for memory consolidation, and wake-up exercise.

14:00 — 16:00
Creative Arts, Coding & Global Studies

Fine motor skills through art, logic through coding, empathy through world cultures — rotating through the week.

16:00 — 17:00
Inventor's Lab & Showcase Prep

Taking things apart, testing ideas, and preparing for Friday's Grand Showcase — recorded for parents.
